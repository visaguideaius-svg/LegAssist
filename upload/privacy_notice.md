## 1. What data we collect

### 1.1 Data you provide directly

We may collect the following types of data when you use the Platform:

- **Account data**:  
  - Email address  
  - Password (stored in hashed form)  
  - Display name (optional)  
  - Language preference (Arabic/English)

- **Usage data**:  
  - Questions you ask the AI assistant  
  - Topics you view  
  - Search queries  
  - Device and browser information (IP address, user agent, approximate location)

- **Contact and lawyer referral data (if you use this feature)**:  
  - Name  
  - Email address  
  - Phone number (optional)  
  - Brief description of your legal issue (in Arabic or English)  
  - Preferred language for communication

- **Privacy consents**:  
  - Records of your consent choices (e.g., data processing, sensitive data, marketing)

### 1.2 Data we collect automatically

When you use the Platform, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

- IP address and approximate location (e.g., city-level, based on IP)  
- Device type, operating system, and browser  
- Pages visited, time spent, and actions taken (e.g., clicking “Talk to a lawyer”)  
- Logs of errors and performance data

We do **not** require you to provide:

- National ID numbers  
- Full ID scans  
- Highly sensitive family, health, or criminal details  

If you voluntarily share such data in free-text fields, we advise you not to do so. We will process only what is necessary to respond to your query and may redact or ignore overly sensitive details.

## 2. Why we process your data (purposes)

We process your personal data only for specific, legitimate purposes, including:

1. **Providing the legal knowledge service**  
   - Answering your questions via the AI assistant  
   - Showing you relevant legal topics and guides  
   - Improving search and classification of legal issues  

2. **Security and fraud prevention**  
   - Detecting and preventing abuse, attacks, and unauthorized access  
   - Investigating security incidents  

3. **Analytics and product improvement**  
   - Understanding which topics are most used  
   - Identifying gaps in our content (e.g., unanswered questions)  
   - Measuring whether users can move from a general problem description to clear next steps  

4. **Lawyer referral (if you use it)**  
   - Storing your contact request to a lawyer  
   - Enabling the lawyer or platform team to contact you regarding your request  

5. **Legal compliance**  
   - Complying with Jordanian laws and regulations, including the PDPL  
   - Responding to lawful requests from authorities  

We do **not** sell your personal data to third parties.

## 3. Legal basis for processing (PDPL alignment)

Under Jordan’s PDPL, we rely on the following bases for processing:

- **Performance of a contract**:  
  Processing necessary to provide the Platform and its services to you (e.g., answering questions, managing your account). [web:5][web:10][web:13][web:15]

- **Legitimate interests**:  
  Processing for security, fraud prevention, analytics, and service improvement, where this does not override your rights and interests. [web:5][web:10][web:13]

- **Consent**:  
  For specific purposes where required by law or where we choose to seek consent, such as:  
  - Processing sensitive personal data (if ever needed)  
  - Certain marketing communications (if we offer them)  

You can withdraw consent at any time via your account settings or by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. [web:5][web:10][web:15]

## 4. Data minimization and special categories

We apply **data minimization** principles:

- We collect only data that is necessary for the stated purposes.  
- We avoid collecting sensitive data (health, family details, criminal matters, national numbers) unless strictly necessary.  
- If you share sensitive data voluntarily, we:  
  - Do not require it for basic use of the Platform.  
  - Advise you not to share more than needed.  
  - Limit access to such data to authorized personnel only.

Under the PDPL, processing of sensitive personal data generally requires **explicit consent** or another specific legal basis. We do not intentionally collect sensitive data as a condition of using the core legal knowledge features. [web:5][web:10][web:13][web:15]
